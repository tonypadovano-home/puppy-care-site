# Puppy Care — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Puppy Care helps a household keep track of a puppy's potty trips, meals, naps and care. This policy explains what
the app stores, where, and who can see it.

## What we store
- **Your sign-in:** your email address and password (the password is stored only in scrambled form).
- **Your household:** its name and time zone, and the name each member chooses to show to the others.
- **Your puppy:** name, birthday and breed.
- **The care log:** each entry's time, type (for example pee, poop, meal, nap), inside or outside, stool rating,
  weight, short notes, and which household member logged it.
- **Care settings:** health and vet reminders, vet questions, and routine settings.

## What we don't do
- No ads.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.
- No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
- We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with anyone for marketing.

## Where it's stored
Your data is stored with Supabase, our database host, on servers in the United States (US East). A copy is also
kept on your phone so the app works offline. Reminders and timers are set on your phone and are not sent to us.

## Who can see it
Only the members of your household. You choose who joins, using an invite code, and owners can remove members.

## Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time from the app: **Family → Delete my account**. Before deleting, you can
export your household's data.
- Your sign-in and your name are deleted. Entries you logged stay in your household's history, shown as "Someone else".
- If you are your household's only owner, deleting your account deletes the whole household, its puppies and its log
  for everyone in it. You can make someone else an owner first to keep it going.
Deleted data may remain in routine database backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

## Children
Puppy Care is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
